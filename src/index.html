How to read LagLens

One pretrained Diffusion Policy pushed a T-block to a goal from the same 50 initial states under 46 execution schedules (strategy × inference latency d × execution horizon h; 1 step = 100 ms) plus one noise replicate of Sync d=0 h=8 that shows how many outcomes flip by chance alone. Every predicted action chunk was logged, so you can see which plan every executed action came from.

  1. A: pick a reference R (shift-click) and a candidate C (click). Colour = the chosen metric; switch to Δ vs R to see the change.
  2. B: each row is one initial state. vermillion = C fails where R succeeds, blue = C rescues it. Click a row to open that episode.
  3. C: R and C replayed side by side. The fan is the chunk being executed: solid = actually executed, dashed = thrown away, dotted grey = the next chunk still being computed, grey dashed = the previous chunk's unused tail.
  4. D: each bar is a chunk's executed span, the thin line before it is the wait for inference, and vermillion ticks are seams (how far the new plan jumped away from the old plan). Brush to focus a time window; hover a bar to see its fan in C.
  5. E: one row per initial state, grouped by how its outcome changed from R to C. The black dot is the value under R, the orange dot under C (default: largest seam gap). If the orange dots of the “C fails” group sit to the right, the failures came with bigger seams. Click a group title or brush rows to filter B.

Keyboard: ←/→ step, space play/pause, Esc clear brush.
