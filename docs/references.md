## Data
* All rollout data in `data/` and `src/data/` were **generated by us** with `scripts/generate_rollouts.py` (PushT) and `scripts/generate_reach.py` (Reach-in-time); no external dataset.
* Reach-in-time (`scripts/reach_task.py`) is our own task and policy: it reuses only the PD-controller constants of gym-pusht (k_p = 100, k_v = 20, 10 ms sub-steps); the policy (`models/reach_policy.pt`) was trained by us on scripted demonstrations with diffusers' DDPM/DDIM schedulers.
